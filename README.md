## The Problem

Every underwater ROV, pan-tilt camera, robotic arm, and rotating platform uses **slip rings** to transmit power and signals across rotating joints. Slip rings are:

- **High maintenance** — brushes wear out, contact degrades over time
- **Unreliable** — signal noise increases, intermittent connections
- **Expensive** — quality slip rings cost $50–500+ per unit
- **Bulky** — take up valuable space in compact assemblies

This is a **billion-dollar pain point** across ROV, robotics, surveillance, and industrial automation markets.

## The Solution

Route all wiring through the **hollow axis** of the rotating joint. No sliding contacts. No brushes. No wear.

| | Slip Ring | Axis-Centered Wiring (Ours) |
|---|---|---|
| Wear | Brush friction, degrades over time | **None** — wires are static |
| Maintenance | Regular brush replacement | **Zero** |
| Signal Quality | Noise from sliding contact | **Clean, stable** |
| Cost | $50–500+ per unit | **Standard cable + connector** |
| Lifespan | Limited by brush wear | **Limited only by cable fatigue** |
| Rotation | Continuous 360° | **Continuous 360°** |
| Waterproofing | Complex sealing needed | **Bellow seal (industrial-proven)** |

## Key Design Features

1. **Axis-Centered Wiring** — All power and signal cables pass through the hollow shaft. No slip ring. No commutator. Zero rotational contact points. Works with power, analog, digital, fiber optic.

2. **Bellow Seal** — Industrial-grade bellow provides waterproof sealing at the rotation joint. Proven in automotive, aerospace, and heavy machinery. Far more reliable than flexible membranes or O-rings.

3. **2:1 Gear Transmission** — Compact gear ratio balances torque and speed for propulsion applications.

4. **Multi-Medium Adaptable** — Designed for underwater ROVs, but the same principle works for:
   - 🌊 **Underwater** — ROVs, AUVs, underwater manipulation
   - 🚁 **Aerial** — pan-tilt cameras, gimbal systems
   - 🤖 **Terrestrial** — robotic arms, rotary joints
   - 🏭 **Industrial** — rotary platforms, cable management
